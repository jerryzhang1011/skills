# Synthetic examples

## Practical inquiry

Task: Ask a repair shop for a quote to replace a laptop battery, whether they have it in stock, and whether the work can be finished in one day. Laptop model is unknown.

Hello,

I would like to replace the battery in my [laptop model]. Could you please provide a quote and let me know if you have the battery in stock?

Would it be possible to complete the replacement in one day?

Thank you,
Jerry

## Chinese institutional inquiry

我想咨询一下活动报名的具体要求。请问报名需要准备哪些材料？是否可以在线完成报名？

Do not treat this synthetic Chinese example as evidence of a verified casual Chinese voice.
