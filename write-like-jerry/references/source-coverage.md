# Evidence scope

This initial profile is based on a limited review of author-attributed sent correspondence. It stores only abstract patterns and limitations. No raw emails, source URLs, recipients, account addresses, attachments, or message identifiers are included.

## Confidence

Three full messages informed the profile: a practical availability/cost inquiry, an interview confirmation, and professional initial outreach. Additional message previews suggested candidate patterns. This is a small sample, not an exhaustive assessment. Sent status does not establish unaided human authorship; polished messages may have been assisted or templated.

- Best supported: purpose-first everyday English email; polite concrete requests; short paragraphs; concise confirmations; simple closings.
- Supported with narrower evidence: formal initial outreach and interview replies.
- Tentative: formal Chinese institutional requests, based primarily on previews; avoid overgeneralizing.
- Not established: casual Chinese chat, long-form reports, essays, presentations, humor, social posting, or a distinctive literary voice.

## Selection boundaries

Exclude automated summaries, test messages, stock consent language, copied forms, other people's quotations, forwarded bodies, and correspondence whose authorship is unclear. Use neither private factual substance nor confidential identifiers in future drafts. Adapt audience modes rather than averaging all messages into one level of formality. The bundled examples are synthetic and do not quote private messages.
